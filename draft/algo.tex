\section{Typing Algorithm}

There are two judgements:
\begin{align*}
    &\Gamma \vdash [H][F] \typeinfer [H'][ e ~|~ F']
    \\&\Gamma \vdash [H][F] \typeinfer [H' ~|~ e][F']
    \\&\Gamma \vdash [H][A][F] \typeinfer [H']e[F']
\end{align*}
